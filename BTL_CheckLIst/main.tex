\row{06}{Add pipe clamps and preheaters. Fasteners: \textbf{M2.2$\times$10 BOSSARD 3772865, M2.2$\times$14 BOSSARD 3772867 and M3$\times$16} CBSTSE3-16, with \textbf{M3 nuts with nylon threadlock inserts BOSSARD 1397176}.}
\row{07}{Add the service plate. Retain the \textbf{fiber holder, rollers and L profiles}; do not lose these components. Check service plate has the grounding insert nut SSFH-315SF-SUS}
\row{08}{Check the fiber holder and rollers are in the correct location for the service plate (\textbf{M2.2$\times$5 BOSSARD 1727702}); fit / check L profiles (\textbf{M2$\times$4}).}
\row{09}{After adding the service plate, connect the fiber to it and connect / secure the cables with \textbf{M3 standoffs}.}
\row{10}{Add manifold holders to the service plate (\textbf{M2.2$\times$6 BOSSARD 1727796}).}
\row{11}{Add Airex with double-sided Kapton; add \textbf{four single-sided Kapton pieces to the edges}. \textbf{Do not press on the LYSO.}}
\row{12}{Before installation, clean the intermediate-beam grooves with isopropyl alcohol. Clean the beams in the BTST as well. Apply PTFE lubricant (if not done previously and with small nozzle,  not 1m away from the groove, only in the groove) to both the intermediate beams and BTST beams, and to the sides of the Bosch profiles before fitting the intermediate beams and sliders. Add intermediate beams to the Bosch profiles; check the profile nuts are correctly rotated and properly seated in the grooves. Check screws for intermediate beam are not lifting the black adapters or pushing against the profiles.}
\row{13}{Start removing the ears from the Bosch profiles. \textbf{Leave the last ear at the bottom in the pipe region.}}
\row{14}{Readjust the intermediate beams to the edge of the profile in the pipes region.}
\row{15}{Add adapters to the BTST beams and add the pulling tool (\textbf{M2.5$\times$8}).}
\row{16}{Add the \textbf{four tray spacers}.}

\row{28}{Put the insertion tool back at the bottom.}
